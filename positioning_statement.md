# Product positioning

Kivi should become a dependable memory of what you have deliberately said, helping you recover past work, understand what changed, and prepare the next useful response. It should remember relevant facts, episodes, and explicit preferences within their proper scope, while treating drafts, quotations, uncertainty, and changing plans carefully. In Hey Kivi, that understanding should produce useful answers and drafts with inspectable sources. Ordinary dictation should preserve your intended meaning. Trust comes from visible evidence, honest uncertainty, and corrections or forgetting that reliably change future behavior.
